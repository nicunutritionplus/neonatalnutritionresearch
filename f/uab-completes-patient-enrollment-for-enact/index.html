UAB completes patient enrollment for ENACT

A total of 102 premature infants born between 28 and 32 weeks of gestation were randomized to receive either higher volume enteral feeds (60-80 ml/ kg/day) or typical enteral nutrition (20-30 ml/kg/day) within the first 36 hours after birth.
